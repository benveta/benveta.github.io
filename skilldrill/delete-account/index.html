Delete your SkillDrill account

SkillDrill · developed by BenVeta

You can delete your SkillDrill account at any time. Deleting it removes your account, your name, your email address, your years of experience and the progress saved in the app. It cannot be undone.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open SkillDrill and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm with your password or your Google account.

Your account and data are deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email us at rahulbenprimary@gmail.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We delete the account and its data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted and what is kept

Deleted: your sign-in account, email address, name, years of experience and your profile record. Progress, notes and bookmarks are stored only on your device and are removed from it when you delete the account in the app, or when you uninstall the app.

Kept by others: advertising data held by Google AdMob is controlled by Google under its own policies.

Read more in our privacy policy.
